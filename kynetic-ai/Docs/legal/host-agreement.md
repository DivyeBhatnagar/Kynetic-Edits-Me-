# Kynetic AI — Host Hardware Marketplace Agreement

**Last Updated**: July 24, 2026

This Host Agreement governs your participation as a Hardware Host ("Host") on the Kynetic AI platform.

---

## 1. Hardware Monetization & Revenue Split

- Hosts set their hourly rental rates or accept dynamic marketplace pricing.
- **Revenue Split**: Hosts receive **85% of all gross rental revenue** generated by their hardware nodes. Kynetic AI retains a **15% platform commission** covering payment processing, security isolation, AI routing, and marketplace operations.
- **Payout Schedule**: Host payouts are disbursed monthly via UPI (for Indian hosts) or Stripe Connect (globally), subject to statutory tax withholdings.

---

## 2. Host Responsibilities & Availability

- Hosts agree to maintain a minimum uptime of **98.0%** for listed hardware nodes.
- Unannounced host disconnections mid-rental will degrade the Host's reputation score (`reputation_scores`) and may incur partial compensation debits.
- Hosts must run official, unmodified Kynetic Host Agent software (`kynetic-host-agent`).

---

## 3. Security & Liability Protection for Hosts

- **Hardware Protection**: Developer workloads run inside strictly isolated Firecracker MicroVMs or non-privileged Docker containers. Developers cannot access the Host's physical operating system, root files, local LAN network, or BIOS/firmware.
- **Prohibited Host Inspection**: Hosts must not attempt to intercept, sniff, or inspect memory or network traffic of developer instances. Attempted host-side inspection results in immediate host de-registration and payout forfeiture.
- **Power & Wear Limitations**: Hosts acknowledge that AI/ML workloads may operate hardware at high GPU/CPU utilisation and accept normal operational wear and tear.

---

## 4. Statutory Tax Withholding (TDS / 1099)

- Indian Hosts are subject to **Section 194O TDS withholding** (1.0% with verified PAN, 20.0% without valid PAN).
- US Hosts are subject to Form 1099-NEC annual reporting if cumulative annual earnings exceed $600 USD.
